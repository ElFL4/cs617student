Boston Crime Statistics Story: Shooting Incidents

By Elley :D

This is is visualization of shooting incident data collected in Boston within different divisions of time: hours, days, weeks, months, and years.

The first chart covers how the amount of incidents within an hour may or may not change throughout a day.

The second chart covers how the amount of incidents within a day of the week may or may not change depending on what day of the week it is.

The third chart covers how the amount of incidents within a month may or may not change depending on what month it is.

The fourth chart shows the amounts of incidents per year from 2023 to (the beginning of) 2026.
